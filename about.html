About Benzos Unchained

Support built by people who understand.

We started Benzos Unchained because we know what it feels like to face benzodiazepine recovery alone. Our mission is simple: connect you with real people, practical guidance, and a community that gets it, every step of the way.

Why We Started This

Benzodiazepine recovery is hard enough without doing it in isolation. We built Benzos Unchained to be the support we wished existed: a space led by people with lived experience, backed by practical coaching, where you're never made to feel like a burden for asking questions.

  • Peer-led support from people who've walked this path
  • A judgment-free space to ask questions and be heard
  • Practical, actionable guidance, not just information
  • A community that shows up with you, session after session

Common Questions

  • Is this only for parents?

    No. It’s for anyone who loves someone struggling with benzodiazepine use or recovery — spouses, partners, family members, and close friends are all welcome.

  • What will we talk about?

    We discuss how to better understand what they’re going through, how to support them without losing yourself, and how to find hope and healing as a family.

  • Do I need to have a member join?

    Not at all. You’re welcome to join on your own. Many of our members are parents and loved ones who want support, guidance, and connection with others in similar situations.

2. Consult a Qualified Healthcare Professional

Always seek the advice of a licensed physician, psychiatrist, or other qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition, substance dependency, tapering protocol, or withdrawal.

3. Risks of Benzodiazepine Withdrawal & Tapering

Discontinuing or rapidly reducing benzodiazepines can cause severe, life-threatening withdrawal symptoms, including seizures. Do not modify your medication dose or stop taking any prescription medication without the direct supervision and guidance of a qualified healthcare provider.
